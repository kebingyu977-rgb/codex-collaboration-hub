## YYYY-MM-DD | TASK-YYYYMMDD-NN | Short task title

- From: Codex / person / computer
- To: Codex / person / computer
- Status: `ready` | `blocked` | `done`
- Goal: What decision or deliverable this task supports.
- Completed: What was actually done.
- Output: Exact file paths, links, sheet ranges, or artifact IDs.
- Evidence: Sources used and their locations.
- Decisions: Approved choices and their reasons.
- Open questions: What remains uncertain.
- Next action: One concrete next step and the owner.
- Risks / blockers: Constraints that must not be ignored.
